FAQ and Resources

Common questions from property owners, construction teams, and municipalities, plus a starter list of organizations and documents to complete for launch.

FAQ

Can we remove a nest if it causes nuisance?

Active nests are generally protected. Check legal requirements before any intervention.

How can we reduce droppings near entrances?

Install collection boards and adjust pedestrian routes where possible.

Who should be contacted first during renovation planning?

Start with municipal/cantonal authorities and an ecological advisor when nests may be affected.

Can new buildings still support house martins?

Yes. Design details such as nest cups and sheltered facade points can be integrated from the start.
