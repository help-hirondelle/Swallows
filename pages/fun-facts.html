Swallow Facts

Short, memorable facts for students, families, and curious visitors walking the trail.

Did You Know?

Mud Is Essential

Swallows need mud to build nests. In sealed cities, a simple clay-rich wet patch can make a real difference.

They Return Each Spring

Swallows often return to former nesting places, so protecting existing sites is critical.

150,000 Flies

One pair of barn swallows may need around 150,000 flies, about 1 kg of food, to raise a brood.

Two Species, Different Homes

House martins nest on facades under overhangs; barn swallows prefer barns, stables, hangars, and semi-open rooms.

A City That Welcomes Swallows Welcomes Biodiversity

Small changes on buildings and nearby green spaces can protect nests, reduce conflict, and support urban nature.
