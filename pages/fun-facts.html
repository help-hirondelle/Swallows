Did You Know?

Mud Architects

House martins build cup-like nests by layering tiny mud pellets one by one.

Long-Distance Travelers

They travel huge seasonal distances and still return close to familiar breeding zones.

Insect Hunters

Swallows catch flying insects in fast aerial loops, helping naturally regulate insect numbers.

Neighbouring Birds

They often settle near people, turning buildings into shared spaces for nesting and daily life.
