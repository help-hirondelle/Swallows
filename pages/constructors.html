Guidance for Constructors and Architects

Most bird-related conflicts can be reduced when ecological considerations are embedded at concept stage rather than treated as late compliance work.

Design Integration Points

Facade Zones

Identify ledges and protected corners where nest cups can be integrated without visual clutter.

Roof Edge Details

Retain or recreate overhang characteristics that support stable nest placement and weather protection.

Access & Maintenance

Design safe maintenance access and install droppings boards where circulation paths are sensitive.

Project Workflow Recommendations

Phase Recommended Step Output
Pre-design Site scan for existing nests and nearby colonies Baseline ecology note
Permit preparation Coordinate with municipality and canton on protected-species handling Compliance checklist
Detailed design Embed nest modules and nuisance mitigation details in drawings Approved construction package
Execution Sequence facade and roof works outside sensitive nesting windows Risk-reduced site plan

Delivery Tip

Include one dedicated biodiversity page in your tender/permit set. This avoids last-minute redesign and keeps cost, legal, and scheduling discussions transparent.
