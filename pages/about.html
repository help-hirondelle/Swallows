The Project

Help Hirondelle translates swallow conservation into practical coexistence guidance for residents, building owners, schools, construction teams, and communes in Lausanne and Vaud.

Coexistence in Practice

This project supports concrete ways for people and swallows to share the same urban spaces.

About Us

We are students from EPFL and ECAL, working together on this project in the context of the Design for Sustainability course (HUM-398).

Our focus is coexistence: helping people and swallows share urban spaces through practical design choices that support biodiversity.
