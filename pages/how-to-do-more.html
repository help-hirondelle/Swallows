Take Action for Swallows

Choose the route that matches your situation: resident, owner, renovation team, commune, or someone who found a bird.

Choose Your Situation

Residents

Live With an Active Nest

Do not remove or block nests. Manage droppings with boards, cleaning plans, and clear building communication.

Owners

Protect Colonies Before Work

Inventory nest sites, monitor them regularly, and account for colonies before renovation, sale, or demolition.

Construction

Check Before Renovation

Look for nests before work. Plan outside the breeding season and restore access before birds return.

Communes

Map, Prioritize, Coordinate

Update colony inventories, define maintain/reinforce/create zones, and use permits to prevent conflicts early.

Green spaces

Support Insects and Mud

Use meadows, hedges, wetlands, ponds, orchards, permeable surfaces, and moist clay-rich mud zones.

Emergency

If You Find a Bird

Injured adults need wildlife care. If an uninjured young bird has fallen, returning it to the nest is often best.

Before Work on a Building

Swallows and their nests are protected. Check for nests before renovation, demolition, or facade work. If active nests are present, plan work outside the breeding season and restore access before birds return.

Best work window: late September to early April

Most Useful First Steps

  • Protect existing colonies before adding new artificial nests.
  • Reduce droppings conflict with maintained boards, not nest removal.
  • Create moist clay-rich mud access near nesting sites during spring.
  • Coordinate measures at commune scale so action is not too late or fragmented.
