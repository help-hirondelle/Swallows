Guidance for House Owners

House owners and managers can often reduce nuisance and support protected birds at the same time by combining practical maintenance planning with simple mitigation tools.

Priority Actions

Observe First

Track where birds are nesting and identify high-conflict zones around doors, balconies, and walking paths.

Protect Active Nests

Avoid removing or blocking active nests. Plan interventions outside the nesting period when legally allowed.

Reduce Nuisance

Install droppings collection boards and align cleaning schedules with residents and caretakers.

Owner Checklist

Before renovation:
  • Document current nests and likely return spots.
  • Ask your commune/canton which approvals are needed.
  • Inform tenants about planned timelines and wildlife considerations.
During operation:
  • Maintain collection boards and keep shared zones clean.
  • Log recurring complaints to prioritize targeted mitigation upgrades.
  • Use simple notice boards to explain why nests are protected.

Legal Reminder

This page is informational only. Always verify specific obligations with current federal and cantonal guidance before physical intervention on nests or facades.
