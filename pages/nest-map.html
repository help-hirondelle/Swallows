Station Highlights

Station 1: Urban Rooflines

Look for overhangs and rough surfaces where nest attachment is easier and shelter is better.

Station 2: Conflict Zones

Observe how entrances and walkways can create droppings conflicts if no board is installed below nests.

Station 3: Coexistence Solutions

Discover simple interventions, like boards and artificial nests, that improve outcomes for birds and people.
