Community guide: why mapping matters

Existing colonies should be inventoried and monitored regularly. If data are missing or older than five years, a new inventory helps communes decide where to maintain, reinforce, or create nesting opportunities.

How communes can use this trail map

Maintain

Protect strong existing colonies and make sure renovation or cleaning work does not accidentally remove them.

Reinforce

Support fragile, declining, or recently abandoned sites with mud access, artificial nests, and conflict management.

Create

Identify suitable buildings where new nesting opportunities could be added near existing swallow activity.

Monitor

Track installed nesting solutions, occupancy rates, complaints, cleaning interventions, and participating sites.
