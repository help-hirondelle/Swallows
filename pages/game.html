Flappy Swallow

Keep the swallow flying through the city while remembering what real swallows need: safe nesting places, mud, and insects.

Play, then look around

The game is simple, but the real challenge is urban design: fewer barriers, more insect habitat, and buildings that leave space for returning swallows.

What the game represents

Obstacles

The pipes stand in for the urban barriers swallows face: sealed openings, smooth facades, and poorly timed renovation work.

Energy

Real swallows spend energy finding insects and mud. A city with more green space and wet soil makes breeding less costly.

Mini-game

Tap, click, or press Space/Arrow Up to keep the swallow flying through gaps.
