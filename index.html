Help Hirondelle

Helping people and swallows share cities with fewer conflicts and better design.

Important: Do not remove active nests. Use protection boards and local guidance to solve hygiene concerns without harming breeding birds.

Why It Matters

House martins depend on buildings for nesting, so facade choices and maintenance decisions directly affect their survival in Lausanne and Vaud.

  • Designing nesting-friendly facades is easier than retrofitting later.
  • Simple solutions can reduce complaints while protecting birds.
  • Education increases local acceptance and long-term success.
