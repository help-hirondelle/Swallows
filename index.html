Project focus: Lausanne and Vaud

House Martins Are Declining. Coexistence Must Be Designed.

In Lausanne and the canton of Vaud, house martin decline is linked to the way cities are built and maintained: smoother facades, fewer overhangs, less mud, fewer insects, and management practices that remove nesting options. Droppings beneath nests are one of the main conflict points, so this project combines conservation with practical hygiene, maintenance, and aesthetic management. HELLOOOOO
